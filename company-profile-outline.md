### Slide 2: About Us
**Headline:** We turn questions into victories.

Quorvin is a strategic intelligence partner specializing in AI, Data, and Web Development consulting. We help businesses navigate complexity — transforming raw data into structured insight, and insight into decisive action.

Our name comes from Latin: Quaerere (to seek) and Vincere (to conquer). Every engagement follows this philosophy — we seek the right questions, structure the path, and deliver results that matter.
